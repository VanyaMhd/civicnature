About CivicNature

CivicNature is a civic reporting platform empowering Sulaymaniyah citizens to document, report, and track urban infrastructure issues and nature sightings — from broken roads to rare species — contributing to a more sustainable and liveable city.

Problem Background

Sulaymaniyah, like many growing cities, faces persistent challenges: potholes, failed street lighting, drainage failures, illegal waste dumping, and the quiet destruction of urban green spaces — parks left to deteriorate, trees cut without authorisation, public gardens neglected.

At the same time, the surrounding highlands are home to rare and endangered species including the Wild Tulip, Persian Cyclamen, Eastern Imperial Eagle, and Spur-thighed Tortoise — all threatened by urban expansion and environmental degradation.

The core problem is the absence of a transparent, structured channel for citizens to report these issues, document nature sightings, and hold authorities accountable.

SDG 11 Alignment — Specific Targets

🏙️ Target 11.3 — Inclusive and Sustainable Urbanisation

SDG 11.3 calls for participatory, integrated, and sustainable human settlement planning. By enabling citizens to actively report, track, and upvote urban issues, CivicNature embodies inclusive civic participation in urban management — putting residents at the centre of city sustainability and giving communities a direct voice in how their city is managed.

🌿 Target 11.6 — Reduce the Environmental Impact of Cities

This target calls on governments and communities to reduce the adverse environmental impact of cities, with particular attention to air quality, waste management, and access to green and natural spaces. CivicNature enables citizens to report open waste burning, illegal dumping, and environmental pollution, creating a community-driven data layer that supports environmental monitoring and accountability.

🌳 Target 11.7 — Provide Access to Safe, Inclusive Green Spaces

This target requires cities to provide universal access to safe, inclusive, and accessible green and public spaces. CivicNature's nature reporting module allows users to document the condition of green spaces, report illegal tree cutting and habitat destruction, and flag deteriorating park facilities — contributing to community oversight of the urban-natural environment.

Key Features

🏙️🌿 Dual Reporting Modes

Switch between Urban Issue and Nature Report modes. Nature mode reveals a species name field and tips for documenting wildlife and rare plants.

📸 Photo Reporting

Attach a photo from your camera or gallery — visual proof makes reports impossible to dismiss.

📊 Public Dashboard

Filterable by category, searchable by keyword, sortable by date, votes, or severity. Includes a real-time bar chart of reports by category.

🗺️ Interactive Map

Urban and nature pins use distinct colour schemes — immediately distinguishing infrastructure issues from environmental sightings.

👍 Community Upvoting

Citizens signal which issues matter most. Urgency rises from the community itself.
